Classes

One path from first move to first trophy

Every student starts with a short level check. From there they join a small group at the right level, move up after a promotion test, and get a certificate at each step.

♙︎

Level 1

Basic

For complete beginners from age 5 and anyone returning to the game.

  • How every piece moves, captures and promotes
  • Check, checkmate, stalemate and castling
  • First tactics: forks, pins, simple mates
  • Writing moves in chess notation

Level 2

Intermediate

For players who know the rules and want to win more games.

  • Opening principles and a starter repertoire
  • Combinations and calculation training
  • Essential endgames: king and pawn, rook
  • Playing with a clock, first rated games
OnlinePhysicalTournament ready

Level 3

Advanced

For competitive players preparing for national and FIDE-rated events.

  • Deep opening preparation by repertoire
  • Middlegame plans and positional play
  • Analysis of your own tournament games
  • Rules and arbiter guidance for competition

Questions

Before you enrol

Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp. We usually reply the same day.

What age can my child start?+

Children can start the Basic level from about age 5. There is no upper age limit, and adult beginners are welcome in their own groups.

How do online classes work?+

Classes run live on video with a shared board. Students need a laptop or tablet, a stable connection and a free online chess account, which we help set up.

How do students move up a level?+

At the end of each level the coach gives a short promotion test. Passing it earns a certificate and a place in the next level's batch.

Do students play in tournaments?+

Yes. Intermediate and Advanced students are prepared for academy events and outside tournaments, including how to use a clock and record moves.

What are the fees?+

Fees depend on the level, the format and group or private classes. Message us on WhatsApp for the current fee list.
